About this simulation

What it is

An educational tool for interviewing figures at the heart of a bioethical dilemma. You ask questions; the figure answers from within their own context and the knowledge of their time.

How to start

Type the name of a figure — a specific person (e.g. "He Jiankui", "Christiaan Barnard", "a physician in the Tuskegee study") or a role (e.g. "a pandemic triage physician", "a member of a hospital ethics committee").

Three phases

Interview (questions 1–5): You interview the figure; from around question 4 the tone sharpens.

Turn the tables (questions 6–13): The figure turns the questions on you and your own coming practice.

Debrief: An instructor (not the figure) reflects with you Socratically. Starts automatically after question 13, or type /debrief / press End & reflect.

Saving & exporting

The conversation is saved in your browser and restored when you reopen. Use Export transcript to save it. Click New session to start fresh.

Prototype adapted from the "Psychology of Evil" Historical Interrogator by Prof. Tom Gumpel, The Hebrew University. Configurable for other disciplines via the ⚙ Setup panel.
